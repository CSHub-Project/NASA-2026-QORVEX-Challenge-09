# Fyrora judge scorecard

Score each criterion from 1–5 only after opening the linked proof. A score without proof is a rehearsal opinion, not a readiness signal.

| Criterion | Judge question | Visible proof | Target | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Impact | Who makes a better decision, and what error is reduced? | Western Iberia pilot + customer workflow hypothesis + calendar | 4 | Claims customers, revenue, emergency response or forecasting without evidence |
| Creativity | Is the approach more than a generic dashboard? | Matched-pass hatch, sensor-source transition, guarded calendar | 4 | Raw concatenation presented as harmonization |
| Validity | Is the science technically defensible and falsifiable? | 2020 holdout, locked 2021–2024 tests, three entirely new 2024 regions, bootstrap, subgroup tables, raw hash inventory, one-pass full-swath evidence, sensor-support gap, failed 90% interval calibration and failed EFFIS outcome gate disclosed | 5 | Only aggregate accuracy, random row split, hiding negative validation, or claiming union-cell WAPE proves VIIRS-only deployment |
| Relevance | Does it answer the actual challenge objective? | Region/year/day-night controls plus a real Western Iberia bounding-box/date-range workflow, coverage-matched historical median, and exact AOI CSV export | 5 | Claims global or live arbitrary-AOI coverage when the interactive evidence window is explicitly bounded to Western Iberia 2017–2020 |
| Presentation | Can the story and value be understood quickly? | 90-second Judge Mode, bilingual UI, architecture, honest limits | 4 | Too many metrics before the problem is clear |

## Gate questions

- Can every quantitative statement be found in a committed table?
- Can the presenter explain that `has_matched_coverage=false` means no detection-derived pair, not proven satellite non-coverage or no fire?
- Can the presenter show that the AOI anomaly compares only supported selected-year days with the same calendar days in other available years?
- Can the presenter explain that 2020 was reused during iteration, while the 2024 three-region protocol and gates were committed before opening its data?
- Can the presenter state both sides of the 2024 result: every locked gate passed, while WAPE still exceeded 1 in Africa and Siberia?
- Can the presenter say what failed without defensiveness? The expected answer includes FRP subgroup stability, the 2023 geometry promotion/interval gate, and the EFFIS outcome gate (ρ=0.130 below 0.30; bootstrap CI crosses zero).
- Does the demo finish before three minutes without opening a new tab or download prompt?

## Award positioning

The strongest global-award evidence is **Best Use of Science** and **Best Use of Data**. Local Impact and Storytelling are supporting narratives. Do not claim that targeting more award categories improves the five-criterion judging score.
