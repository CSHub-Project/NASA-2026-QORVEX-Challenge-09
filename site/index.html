Traceable by design

Processing and validation method.

  1. 01

    Match detection passes

    Pair detection-derived Aqua MODIS and S-NPP VIIRS passes by region, date, day/night and time tolerance.

  2. 02

    Share the ground

    Aggregate both sensors to the same 10 km equal-area cells before comparison.

  3. 03

    Guard the future

    Fit on 2017–18 and select on 2019; exclude 2020 from both steps and report iteration history.

  4. 04

    Show uncertainty

    Bootstrap by date, report subgroups, preserve null coverage, and publish machine-readable evidence.
